## Themes worth being able to talk about

- **Measure before you tune.** The retrieval changes are gated on a benchmark
  that lands first; every ranking change comes with a number.
- **Stubs at the seam, real models in one job.** The suite stubs
  `embed_texts` and runs in seconds on every matrix cell; one scheduled job
  loads the real model.
- **The artifact is the test.** The committed sample PDF is checked against
  its generator, the wheel is installed and run in CI, and `AppTest` runs the
  real Streamlit script.
- **Decisions are written down.** `DECISIONS.md` carries the why for every
  choice the spec left open, so each can be defended or reversed on purpose.
